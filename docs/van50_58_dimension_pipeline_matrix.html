Pipeline Audit Stages (6-Stage Autonomous Flow) Discovery, link upgrading, cart verification, enrichment & publishing
  • 1A calendar_harvester.js Schedule Harvester — Discovers candidate events, performance dates, curtain times, and recurrence rules from venue calendars, platform feeds & discovery sources into layer_schedules.json.
  • 1B tier1_hunter.js Link Tier Hunter — Automatically inspects interactive ticketing buttons, embeds, and redirects to locate direct Tier 1 checkout carts (candidate_tier1_url) rather than generic calendar hubs.
  • 2 event_batch_qc.js Live Box Office QC — Headless Chromium agent (Stagehand); navigates live carts, increments quantities, verifies fees + 5% GST (enforcing ≤ $50 CAD cap), and captures cart keyframes into layer_box_office.json.
  • 3 enricher.js Semantic Enricher — Cognitive NLP agent; normalizes production titles, extracts performer lineups, classifies 11 cultural archetypes, extracts age limits, and crafts concise synopses into layer_editorial.json.
  • 4 venue_detailer.js Venue Detailer — Facility grounding AI; verifies physical Metro Vancouver street addresses, geocodes GPS coordinates, maps transit waypoints, environment type, and physical accessibility amenities into data/venues.json (Venue Detailer Facility DB).
  • 5 compile_catalog.js Compiler AI Publisher — Sole authorized publisher; arbitrates multi-layer evidence, enforces budget & geographic invariants, auto-rolls recurring showings, assigns permanent IDs, publishes to events.json, and syncs data.js.
Storage Architecture & Persistence Files Staging layers, core master registries, evidence proofs & web export
  • layer_schedules.json Harvested Schedules — Intermediate staging layer for raw event schedules, multi-day showings, door/curtain times, and recurrence rules discovered by Stage 1A.
  • layer_box_office.json Box Office Evidence — Intermediate staging layer for live verified cart prices, base fares, itemized fees, taxes, booking protocols, and cart proof paths from Stage 2.
  • layer_editorial.json Editorial Metadata — Intermediate staging layer for normalized titles, performer lineups, archetype tags, age limits, and editorial blurbs from Stage 3.
  • venues.json (Venue Detailer DB) Venue Detailer Facility Database — Explicit physical facility ground-truth database maintained by the Venue Detailer AI; stores civic street addresses, GPS pins, operating hours, spend benchmarks, and transit access.
  • events.json Master Catalog — Master active catalog published exclusively by Compiler AI; contains verified cultural events meeting all budget (≤ $50 CAD) and quality criteria.
  • manual_review_queue.json Quarantine Queue — Triage review queue holding candidate cards that fail fatal dealbreaker gates, exceed price ceiling, or await curator policy decisions.
  • archived_events.json Lifetime Event Archive — Permanent historical archive preserving concluded, cancelled, sold-out, or expired events with immutable registration IDs (Event_Num_Perm).
  • proofs/*.webp Visual Proof Anchors — High-resolution keyframe captures of checkout carts, hero headers, and venue signage serving as immutable visual proof.
  • data.js Frontend Web Export — Serialized JavaScript dataset synced from events.json by Compiler AI for instant zero-dependency client-side web application loading.
